Comprehensive Windows-Based ERP & Accounting Solutions

NSC Computers specializes in developing flexible, offline enterprise resource planning (ERP) software designed to streamline business management. Our systems operate natively on Windows platforms, ensuring high speed, data privacy, and continuous offline reliability without relying on active internet connections.

Key Software Capabilities:

  • Flexible Accounting: Complete double-entry accounting with real-time financial reporting and ledger management.
  • Inventory & Stock Control: Live stock tracking, automated reorder alerts, and multi-warehouse management tools.
  • Invoicing & Billing: Fast, customizable invoice generation compliant with local business regulations.
  • Business Management: Centralized dashboard for tracking sales, purchases, and client history.
